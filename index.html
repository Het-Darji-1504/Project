About LTS

LTS was founded in 2020 by a group of passionate food enthusiasts who wanted to bring authentic Mexican flavors to their community. Our mission is to provide delicious, high-quality tacos made with fresh ingredients and traditional recipes. We believe that food is not just about taste, but also about creating memorable experiences for our customers.

When did Tacos first appear in the United States?

Tacos first appeared in the United States in the early 20th century, gaining popularity particularly after World War II.
